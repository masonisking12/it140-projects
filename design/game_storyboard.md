## Theme and Storyline

**Theme:**

The Stolen Mission is an adventure game where the player travels through a village to recover important supplies and information needed to complete a mission.

**Storyline:**

The player begins at Mission Home and must travel through the village to collect six important items before confronting the Thief in the Abandoned Building. The player must collect the Mission Map, Service Supplies, Food Package, Language Guide, Flashlight, and Journal. These items will help the player complete the mission and prepare for the final encounter. The Thief is the Main threat and must be avoided until all six items have been collected.

## Rooms

Project One requires a minimum of eight rooms.

1. Mission Home - Start room
2. Village Square
3. Community Center
4. Marketplace 
5. Library
6. Mountain Trail
7. Chapel
8. Abandoned Building - Villain room

Add more rooms if your design needs them.

## Items

With the minimum eight-room design, Project One requires at least six items.
Every room except the start room and villain room must contain one item.

1. Mission Map
2. Service Supplies 
3. Food Package 
4. Language Guide
5. Flashlight 
6. Journal

If you add rooms beyond the minimum, add an item for every additional room
except the start room and villain room.

## Villain

The Thief is the villain who is hiding in the Abandoned Building. The player must collect all six items before entering the villain's room and confronting the Thief.
